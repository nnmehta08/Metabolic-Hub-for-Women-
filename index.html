Clinical intent

In postmenopausal women with overweight/obesity, key cardiometabolic assessment pillars include blood pressure, lipids, glucose/diabetes status, and adiposity (BMI + waist circumference), integrated with global ASCVD risk, metabolic syndrome clustering, female-specific risk enhancers, and (selected) CAC/Lp(a)/hs-CRP and kidney function.
